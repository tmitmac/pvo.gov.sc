About property valuations

The Property Valuation Office maintains the Square Country valuation system and establishes standards for the preparation and maintenance of valuation information.

Valuation information supports local government rating systems, property administration, government planning and public access to property information.

A valuation is a snapshot of the assessed value of a property at a specified valuation date. It should not be treated as a guaranteed sale price or as a substitute for an independent professional valuation.
